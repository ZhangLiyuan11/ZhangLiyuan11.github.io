About Me

I am Liyuan Zhang (张力元), a PhD Candidate in Southeast University, Nanjing, China, advised by Prof. Xiaolin Fang. Previously, I obtained a Master of Software Engineering from Heilongjiang University, advised by Prof. Yan Yang and Prof. Yong Liu.

My research focuses on multimodal learning and fake news detection, with a particular interest in developing robust detection systems for short video platforms. More recently, my research interests have been moving towards Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and open-world detection.

Please feel free to reach out for potential collaborations or discussions.

Research Interests: Multimodal Learning, Vision-Language Models, Open-World Detection.
